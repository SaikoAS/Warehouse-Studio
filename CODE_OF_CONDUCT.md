# Code of Conduct

Warehouse Studio welcomes contributors of every background and experience level. Be respectful, constructive and patient. Discuss ideas and behavior with evidence, accept feedback, and make room for different perspectives.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, personal attacks and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable. These standards apply to repository issues, pull requests, discussions and project spaces, and when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

The maintainer @SaikoAS is responsible for enforcing this policy. For ordinary moderation concerns, mention @SaikoAS in the relevant thread. For a confidential concern, request a private contact channel without publishing names, personal information or incident details. No dedicated confidential conduct mailbox has been configured yet. Use GitHub's **Report abuse** feature for platform abuse where appropriate.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, give a warning, restrict participation temporarily or ban a participant according to severity and recurrence. Affected participants may ask the maintainer to reconsider through an available private channel. Reports should be handled discreetly; no absolute confidentiality or response-time guarantee is made.
